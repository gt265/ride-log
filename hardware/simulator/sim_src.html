      Things worth trying

      1. Start riding, then press START on the device. Watch distance and moving time count.
      2. Ride through a traffic light: the device shows AUTO-PAUSE and moving time stops.
      3. Press LAP, or set AUTO_LAP_KM to 1, and check the Laps page.
      4. Wake the heart-rate strap and power meter, then look at the Sensors page and zones.
      5. Hold START to save, go to the Device page, hold START for Wi-Fi, and download the ride.
      6. Flip the power switch mid-ride: the e-paper keeps the last picture, but the unsaved ride is lost.
